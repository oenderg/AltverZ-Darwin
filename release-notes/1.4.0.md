**Now on Windows.** PvZ2: Reflourished runs on Windows PCs, with the same settings, keys and saves menu as on the Mac. One setup installs it on both Intel/AMD and ARM PCs, and it updates itself from then on.

- Windows: the first start downloads the game's data (1.3 GB) and, on Intel and AMD PCs, prepares the game for that PC once (about 2 minutes on a recent PC, longer on older ones). Later starts take seconds.
- Windows: the game runs at your display's refresh rate with **Target 60 FPS** on, in a window you can resize or in full screen (F11 or Alt+Enter). The plant you picked follows the pointer, as on the Mac.
- Windows: open **Settings** with `Ctrl+,`, by right-clicking the title bar (or `Alt+Space`), or by right-clicking the game's taskbar button. Both menus also have **Check for Updates**.
- New default keys: seed packets 5 to 8 now sit on `Q` `W` `E` `R`, next to 1 to 4, with `Tab` for fast-forward and `F` for plant food. Keys you set yourself stay as they are.
- Mac: the key labels in levels are now on by default (Settings > Keyboard > **Show keys in levels**).
- Levels that hide the shovel, plant food or power-ups, such as Ancient Egypt Day 23, no longer show labels for them, and their keys no longer pick up the hidden control.
- iPad: in a window on iPadOS 27, the game now resizes to any size you drag it to instead of jumping between a few shapes and sometimes showing the picture stretched.
- iPhone and iPad: a single swipe at the bottom edge no longer leaves the game; it takes a second swipe, as at the top.
