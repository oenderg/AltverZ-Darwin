The first release: **PvZ2: Reflourished 1.4.2, running natively on iPhone, iPad and Mac.** No jailbreak, no JIT, no emulator.

- Plays at your display's refresh rate: 120 Hz on ProMotion iPads.
- Touch, mouse and trackpad, plus keyboard shortcuts on the Mac and on an iPad with a keyboard: `1`-`8` seed packets, `S` shovel, `Space` collects sun and coins, `Esc` pauses.
- On the Mac, the window takes any size, or any shape from 4:3 to 21:9, and you can pick your own keys in Settings.
- The Mac app updates itself, and iPhone / iPad get updates through the AltStore and SideStore source.
