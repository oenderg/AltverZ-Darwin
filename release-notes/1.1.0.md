**Events and limited-time offers now show up.** The Halloween event, the weekly challenge, their badges on the map and the shop's rotating limited-time offers were missing on iPhone, iPad and Mac: the game takes its time from Reflourished's content server, and that time never reached it.

- The game now loads Reflourished's live content at start-up, as on Android, so content updates arrive without a new app version.
- Offline, it uses the content it last downloaded and your device's clock, so events still show.
- The game's log starts fresh at each launch; the previous run's is kept as `pvz2.previous.log`.
