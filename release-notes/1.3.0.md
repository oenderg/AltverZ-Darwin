**See your keys, and where your plant will go.** In a level, each control can show the key that works it, and on the Mac the plant you picked follows the pointer, with a cross on the tile it will land on.

- Key labels: on the Mac, turn on Settings > Keyboard > **Show keys in levels**. On iPad, they appear once you press a key on a hardware keyboard.
- Mac: the selected plant, the shovel or plant food follows the pointer. The cross hides where the plant can't go, such as water without a lily pad. Turn it off, or change the cross's brightness, in Settings > Keyboard.
- Import Quests works: on iPhone and iPad it no longer closes the game, and on the Mac it asks for a file to open instead of where to save one. Pick the quests zip you exported.
